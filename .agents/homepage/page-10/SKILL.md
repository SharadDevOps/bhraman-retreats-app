# Homepage Section: Page 10 — Thoughts for the Journey Within

## 1. Overview
Page 10 (`<section className="blog-section section" id="journal">`) previews thoughtful articles from the Bhraman Journal.

## 2. Key Components & Structure
- **Featured Blog Card (`.featured-blog`)**:
  - `ResponsiveMedia`: Article cover image (`blog/why-choose-bhraman/cover`).
  - Text block: Author name, article title, excerpt summary, and "Read the journal" action linking to `/blog/[slug]`.

## 3. Data & CMS Fields
- `blogLabel`: "From the journal"
- `blogTitle`: "Thoughts for the journey within."
- API: `/api/public/blogs` returning published journal entries.
